## The five programs (all stackable)

### 1. RRSP Home Buyers' Plan (HBP)

- **Withdraw:** up to **$60,000 per person** tax-free from your RRSP
- **Couple total:** $120,000
- **Repayment:** 15 years, starting year 5 after purchase (extended in Budget 2024 for withdrawals in 2022–25)
- **Best for:** buyers with existing RRSP savings
- **Source:** Government of Canada — Home Buyers' Plan (canada.ca/en/revenue-agency)

### 2. First Home Savings Account (FHSA)

- **Contribute:** up to **$8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime** per person
- **Tax treatment:** deductible on the way in AND tax-free on the way out
- **Couple total:** $80,000
- **Best for:** buyers 18+ who don't own a home this year or in the past 4 years
- **Source:** Government of Canada — First Home Savings Account

### 3. Ontario Land Transfer Tax First-Time Buyer Rebate

- **Rebate:** up to **$4,000** on Ontario provincial LTT
- **Best for:** every first-time buyer in Ontario
- **Source:** Government of Ontario — Land Transfer Tax Refund

### 4. Toronto Municipal Land Transfer Tax First-Time Buyer Rebate

- **Rebate:** up to **$4,475** on Toronto MLTT
- **Combined with #3:** up to **$8,475 in LTT rebates** on a Toronto purchase
- **Best for:** first-time buyers purchasing in Toronto proper
- **Source:** City of Toronto — Municipal Land Transfer Tax rebate

### 5. Insured mortgage 30-year amortization

- **Amortization:** up to **30 years** on insured mortgages (raised from 25 in Dec 2024)
- **Applies to:** first-time buyers and new-build purchases
- **Impact:** lower monthly payment (higher lifetime interest — worth modelling)
- **Source:** Department of Finance Canada — 2024 mortgage insurance rule changes

## Sample stack: FTHB couple, Toronto purchase

Household income: $130,000 combined
Purchase price: $700,000
Combined savings: $120,000

**Stack:**
- HBP: $50,000 (both partners partial withdrawal)
- FHSA: $40,000 (both partners lifetime withdrawal)
- Personal savings: $30,000 (kept as reserve)
- **Total down payment: $90,000 (12.9%)**
- **Ontario LTT rebate: $4,000**
- **Toronto MLTT rebate: $4,475**
- **Amortization: 30 years insured**

**Result:** buyers who thought they had $120k for a down payment actually mobilize $98,475 into the deal (down + LTT relief), preserve $30k in reserves, and lock in a 30-year amort that reduces the monthly payment by ~$260/mo vs a 25-year.
