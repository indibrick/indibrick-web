## What you'll get in your Rescue Report

A one-page PDF with:

### 1. Your bank's offer scored

- Percentile: is this above, at, or below broker-channel best?
- 5-year total interest at your bank's rate

### 2. Broker-channel best available

- Top 3 lenders willing to fund your file at renewal
- Their current rates (updated multiple times daily)
- Whether the switch would be insured or uninsured

### 3. Interest delta over the term

- Total interest at your bank's offered rate
- Total interest at broker-channel best
- **Your projected savings — the dollar number that matters**

### 4. Cost to switch

- Legal fees (usually covered by new lender on insured switches)
- Discharge fee from your current lender (typical $270–$400)
- Net switching cost (usually negative — the new lender's cash-back exceeds costs)

### 5. Blend-and-extend option

- Whether your existing lender will offer a blended rate if you request one
- The likely blended rate vs a straight renewal
- Whether the blend is worth taking

### 6. Break-and-refinance option

- Whether breaking early (before maturity) makes sense
- Penalty math (IRD vs 3-month interest)
- Break-even month

### 7. Do-nothing scenario

- What happens if you sign the bank's letter as-is
- 5-year cost of doing nothing

### 8. Recommendation

- Our specific recommendation with reasoning
- Next steps if you want to proceed
- Timeline (rate hold, application, closing)
