## Why a shadow appraisal

Pre-construction units bought in 2020–22 across the GTA are appearing at appraised values below original contract price at final closing in 2024–26. This is a well-documented broker-channel and market analyst observation (Urbanation, Realosophy).

If your lender's appraisal comes in low **at closing**, you have days — not weeks — to bridge the gap. A shadow appraisal, ordered independently at least 60 days before closing, tells you the number early enough to build a real plan.

## What happens next

1. **We route to appraiser** (within 24 hours of receiving this form)
2. **Appraisal ordered** and access coordinated with developer (2 business days)
3. **Report delivered** to you and (optionally) us within 5–7 business days
4. **We build the closing plan** based on the appraisal number:
   - **If appraisal supports contract:** you close normally with your current lender
   - **If appraisal is 5–10% short:** we build a bridge financing plan
   - **If appraisal is 10%+ short:** we assess B-lender workout or documented walk-away
