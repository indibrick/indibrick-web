## Reality check on the Foreign Buyer Ban

The federal Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Act was extended through **January 1, 2027**. This restricts non-Canadian purchases of residential property in most Canadian metropolitan areas. Exemptions exist for permanent residents, temporary residents on work permits (with specific criteria), refugee claimants, and international students meeting narrow requirements.

**Confirm your visa's eligibility before signing any Agreement of Purchase and Sale.** We provide a written eligibility opinion as part of every newcomer file review.

## The four levers most banks miss

### 1. Foreign credit history acceptance

Equifax International and Nova Credit both pull credit histories from India, US, UK, and select other countries. Scotia, RBC, and select monolines will use these reports to underwrite. **This turns a "no credit" file into an "excellent credit" file overnight.** Most branch reps do not know this option exists.

### 2. 12 months rental history as credit proxy

If you've paid rent to a professional landlord in Canada for 12+ months, most A-lenders will accept documented rent as a credit substitute. Bring 12 months of bank statements showing rent debits.

### 3. ITIN mortgages

Two B-lenders (Home Trust and one private) will lend on ITIN (Individual Tax Number) alone, before you have a SIN. Rate premium ~200 bps. Useful bridge for the 3–6 months between arrival and full SIN + employment.

### 4. Higher down payment for better pricing

35%+ down unlocks A-lender pricing at lenders who would otherwise decline on credit or work-permit tenure. If you have the funds abroad, moving them to Canada BEFORE applying dramatically expands your options.

## Documentation checklist

For every newcomer mortgage application:
- PR card OR work/study permit
- Passport
- SIN card (or ITIN if applicable)
- Last 3 months of Canadian bank statements
- Last 2 pay stubs + employer letter
- Foreign credit report if available
- 12 months rental history (if using as credit proxy)
- Landing paperwork (if PR)
- 2 years foreign tax returns (if self-employed)
